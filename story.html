The Atelier Chronicles

The Yaschi Story

Where old-world romanticism meets modern artisanal craftsmanship. A celebration of wildflowers, slow afternoons, and silhouettes that dance in the breeze.

Poetry You Can Wear

Yaschi was founded in response to the rushed pace of modern fast fashion. We longed for the heirloom touch of bygone eras: delicate pintucks sewn by patient hands, tapestries inspired by English cottage gardens, boned bodices that flatter every curve, and lace that feels as light as morning mist.

Every corset, tiered dress, sheer shrug, and botanical talisman in our collection begins with an original sketch and slow, thoughtful fabric sourcing. We believe that what touches your skin should carry intention, gentleness, and beauty.

"We believe clothing is not merely fabric; it is an invitation to inhabit your own daydream with grace, soft strength, and untamed romance."

Our Guiding Ethos

Slow Couture

Crafted in limited, intentional drops to prevent textile waste and ensure impeccable stitchery in every garment.

Botanical Materials

Breathable natural cottons, linens, and floral embroideries that age gracefully with each wearing.

Inclusive Grace

Thoughtfully tailored silhouettes from XS to XL designed to celebrate and honor every feminine form.
