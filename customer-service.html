Frequently Asked Questions

Orders are dispatched from our boutique within 24 hours. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days across metro cities, and 4 to 6 business days for regional pin codes.
We accept returns and exchanges on unworn, unwashed items with original tags within 7 days of delivery. Doorstep pickup is scheduled free of charge, and refunds are processed within 48 hours of return inspection.
Every garment features an interactive Size Chart on its product page with detailed Bust, Waist, and Hip measurements. Our corsets are designed with flexible steel boning and adjustable back lacing allowing a 2-inch custom fit.
We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), and all major Credit and Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay).
